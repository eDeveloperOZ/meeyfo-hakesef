# Party inclusion

A party, list, or faction is included when any condition is met:

1. represented in the current Knesset;
2. appeared in at least two nationwide voting-intention polls in the most recent rolling
   30-day period; or
3. the owner exception: המפלגה הכלכלית.

The Economic Party exception is intentionally public. It changes inclusion only and grants no
exception to source, verification, navigation, or presentation rules.

Election brand, registered party, election list, and parliamentary faction must be modeled
honestly. When the legal identity of a new brand is pending, mark that state in `notes` and do
not invent a registration relationship.

Re-evaluate eligibility on every data refresh. Removal from the current grid requires an
owner-reviewed PR and must preserve historical records.

The poll review must not rely on a single broadcaster. Its documented base includes
nationwide voting-intention polls published by outlets such as N12, חדשות 13, כאן חדשות,
i24NEWS, מעריב and ישראל היום, when the publication identifies the fieldwork or pollster and
the tested party set. A named party counts as an appearance even when it is reported below the
electoral threshold. Two republications of the same fieldwork count once; two distinct
fieldwork dates count as two appearances. Scenario-only combinations are recorded separately
and are not treated as the party's ordinary appearance unless that exact list is the modeled
entity.

The 2026-07-27 review added בל״ד after distinct חדשות 13 polls on 2026-07-08 and 2026-07-15
named it below the threshold; a כאן חדשות poll on 2026-07-19 provided an additional
cross-outlet check.
